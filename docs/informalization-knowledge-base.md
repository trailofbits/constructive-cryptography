# Paper-Literacy & Informalization Knowledge Base

This document is the permanent technical reference for the Constructive Cryptography informalization pipeline in [`cc-informalization`](../README.md). It consolidates:

1. The **baseline PDF-vs-HTML cross-cutting failure taxonomy** (Categories A–D) derived from side-by-side visual audits of **76 deduplicated theorems** across 9 exported bundles (`boneh_shoup_part1`, `verbose_cc`, `prg`, `aead`, `indifferentiability`, `games_hcoeff`, `p0_regressions`, `sha3-wave1`, `sha3-wave2`) compared against 11+ reference cryptographic papers and monographs (Boneh–Shoup Ch. 2–4, Maurer 2011, Maurer–Renner 2016, Banfi Thesis, Maurer–Rüedlinger–Tackmann 2012, Jost–Maurer 2020, ModularKeyedSponge, SpongeIndifferentiability, KeyedSpongeSecurity, and the SHA-3 SequenceMAC monograph).
2. The **12 falsifiable paper-literacy invariants (`L1`–`L12`)** enforced mechanically in [`scripts/check_informalization.py`](../scripts/check_informalization.py).
3. The **remediation architecture and implementation status** across **Wave A**, **Wave B**, and **Wave C**.

> **Zero-Edit Compatibility Constraint:** All improvements to legacy and SHA-3 informalization outputs must work without modifying target theorem source files (`sequence-hash-release/src/` remains strictly unmodified; domain aliases, binders, opacity rules, and section preludes live in companion overlay/export modules or the core informalization engine).

## Part III — Non-Negotiable Paper-Literacy Invariants (`L1`–`L12`)

Every exported `.json` and standalone `.html` bundle (both **`authored`** and **`legacy`** tiers) is checked against these 12 falsifiable invariants in [`scripts/check_informalization.py`](../scripts/check_informalization.py):

| # | Invariant Name | Falsifiable Specification | Mechanical Gate in `scripts/check_informalization.py` | Gate Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** | **Sound Formula Preservation** | Error parameters (`ε`), query budgets (`q`), and operator arguments (`W` in `Wα(G₂)`) present in Lean statements/proofs must never be dropped or glued. | Reject `\(\\cdot\)\^\{` across all reader-facing math/prose (except `epsilonRelaxation(...) := (\cdot)^{\mathrm{error}}` definition equations) and reject glued `\\alpha[A-Za-z]` (`\alphaG_2`). | **`FAIL`** (all tiers) |
| **L2** | **Valid KaTeX / LaTeX Hygiene** | Zero double subscripts (`z_{1}_{2}`, `z_1_2`), zero uninterpolated `${...}` template strings, and zero raw un-delimited `\mathcal`/`\mathbf`/`\mathsf`/`\mathbb`/`[\,` in plain-text inspector fields. | `check_latex_hygiene`: checks math expressions for `_(?:\{[^{}]+\}\|[A-Za-z0-9])_(?:\{[^{}]+\}\|[A-Za-z0-9])`, all prose/math for `_\{\w+\}_\{` and `\$\{[^}]+\}`, and stripped plain-text inspector fields (`roleDescription`, `witnessCard` rows, `proseSummary`, `budgetNote`, `docString`) for raw TeX. | **`FAIL`** (all tiers) |
| **L3** | **Mounted Visuals & Live Inspector Completeness** | Every `dataKey`, `.symbol`, `declKey`, and `referencedKey` must resolve in document/section/local inspectors; all `diagramHtml` / `svgOrHtml` / `figure.html` strings must be free of embedded `<style>` and raw Lean module literals; emitted diagrams must mount in the browser DOM. | `check_inlines` / `check_section_preludes` reject any dangling `symbol.key`, `dataKey`, `data-cadmo-key`, `declKey`, or `referencedKey`; `check_diagram_html` rejects `CategoryTheory.`, `ConstructiveCryptography.`, `SystemAlgebra.`, `{real}`, `{ideal}`; `--playwright` verifies `.katex-error == 0` and mounted diagram SVGs. | **`FAIL`** (all tiers) |
| **L4** | **Section & TOC Structural Integrity** | Every entry carrying `sectionTitle` must carry a non-empty `sectionId`; headings must never double-number (`"1. Section 2.1"`). | Reject `entry.sectionTitle` with missing/null/empty `sectionId`; reject `sectionTitle` or rendered HTML `<h1..h6>` headings matching `^\s*\d+\.\s*(?:Section\|§\|Chapter\|Part)\s*\d`. | **`FAIL`** (all tiers) |
| **L5** | **Honest Justification Dispatch** | Computational (`≃`), statistical (`≈`), and specification-refinement (`⪯`) hops must never be justified `"by elementary algebra"`. | Reject `"by elementary algebra"` on any `calcChain` step whose `relationLatex` contains `\simeq`, `\approx`, or `\preceq`. | **`FAIL`** (all tiers) |
| **L6** | **Bounded DAG Proof Trees (Zero Sibling Re-Expansion)** | A theorem proof must never re-expand the proof body of another top-level `Entry` in the same document (citing a sibling as a leaf or passing call-site premise args is valid; inlining the sibling's internal proof body is forbidden). | Build per-entry body signatures (non-core `originDecl`s and canonical non-boilerplate step/`calcChain` texts) and FAIL if any descendant `ProofNode` with `originDecl` pointing to another `Entry` re-expands that sibling's body inside `children`.
